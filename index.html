1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Tower Build] ("the Game"), developed by [Jiri Navratilik], handles information when you use the Game.

2. Information Stored by the Game

The Game allows you to save your game score and high score. This information is stored locally on your device.

Your locally stored score is used only to provide the functionality of the Game, such as remembering your score or high score.

  • country or approximate location derived from technical information;
  • information about advertisements displayed or interacted with;
  • information about Game sessions and advertising events.

This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, provide personalized advertising.

5. Data Sharing

The Game does not sell your locally stored game score.

Information processed by Unity Ads may be shared with Unity and its advertising and service partners as necessary to provide advertising services, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalized advertising where permitted.

6. Data Security

The Game does not transmit your locally stored game score to our servers.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to their own privacy and security practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game score remains stored locally on your device until it is deleted or overwritten by the Game, or until you remove the Game or its application data using your device settings.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of your game score.

Information processed by Unity Ads is retained according to Unity's applicable privacy policies and retention practices.
